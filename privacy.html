白身 (Baishen) 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-10-05（随 v1.0.6 发布）

一句话：核心检测完全在本地运行，不需要账号，不上传任何数据。 只有当你主动填入激活码并主动开启云同步之后，才会有数据离开你的电脑。

1. 不需要账号时：什么都不会上传

以下功能在完全离线状态下工作，不产生任何网络请求：

  • 读取棋盘落子序列、跑引擎复盘、计算风险分
  • 本地存档与样本库
  • 学习机制（阈值自动搜索）与黑名单
  • 自定义模型（权重包存在本机 IndexedDB）

2. 激活并登录后，会上传什么

项目内容目的
用户标识username、email账户本身
头像avatar（压缩到 256×256）主页显示
账户状态激活状态、设备 ID、最后活跃时间一码一账户、跨设备上限 3 台
样本统计条数、AI 比例等聚合数字主页数字
徽章已授予的徽章类型成就系统
平台痕迹（1.0.6）扩展版还是网页版、每次登录时刻、已推断的国家代码（不存 IP）运维统计，只有管理员可见
消息已读水位（1.0.6）五个分区的最后已读时间红点提示

3. 不会上传什么

  • 不会上传棋谱原始数据——逐手记录留在本机
  • 不会上传检测结果——风险分、每步判定只在本机计算
  • 不会上传本地设置——除非你自己打开云同步
  • 不会上传黑名单——默认不同步，需手动勾选

3.1 关于对手的数据

样本、回放存档与黑名单里带着对手的显示名。开启云同步并勾选对应类别后，这些名字会随之上传——它们是你自己采集的内容，不是我们从服务端读来的。

4. 数据存放在哪里

Supabase（PostgreSQL + Storage）托管，建议选择新加坡（Southeast Asia, Singapore）；实际区域以你使用的部署为准。

5. 保留期

  • 活跃账户：数据一直保留，直到你删除
  • 注销账户：本地数据保留原位，云端数据 30 天后彻底删除
  • 激活码泄露：管理员可批量撤销，撤销不影响本地数据
  • 平台登录流水：目前无单独保留期，随账户数据保留，注销后 30 天内随云端删除
  • 聊天消息：客户端可见窗口为最近 7 天；分享文件的本机副本保留 15 分钟

6. 你的权利

  • 导出：扩展的「导入与导出」面板导出全部本地数据
  • 删除：「我的 → 注销账户」申请删除云端数据（30 天后生效）
  • 更正：「我的 → 修改资料」改用户名、邮箱、头像

7. 未成年人

本扩展面向在线五子棋的成年玩家。若可能包含未成年人，请在使用前取得监护人同意。

8. 第三方

本服务会接触以下第三方，除此之外不向任何第三方发送数据：

  • Supabase —— 托管与存储
  • ipinfo.io —— 登录时由服务端把你的 IP 发给它推断国家代码（IP 本身不落库，见第 10 节）
  • Resend —— 投递验证码邮件与管理员回复邮件，你的邮箱地址与邮件正文会经过它

检测引擎（Rapfi / KataGomo）在本机或你自己配置的地址运行；「自动提问」功能使用你自己填写的 LLM 接口与密钥——这两者都不经由我们。

9. 联系方式

管理员邮箱：mod0207613@qq.com

10. 在你自己的电脑上：哪些是明文

chrome.storage.local 本身不加密。以下内容以明文存在浏览器配置目录，任何能读到该目录的程序都能拿到：

  • 「自动提问」用的 LLM API Key（导出备份会剥离，本机不剥离）
  • 云账户登录令牌（JWT，30 天有效）——它被放在 settings 之外，导出/同步都不会带上；注销、改密码或管理员撤销后即失效
  • 聊天室本机缓存（IndexedDB 库 baishen-cache）——最近 7 天的公共房间消息、分享文件副本（15 分钟）与同步元数据；撤回会从该缓存删除

如果你不信任本机上的其他程序，请不要启用「自动提问」，也不要登录云账户——核心检测功能两者都不需要。

11. 在线状态与国籍的可见范围

「最后活跃时间」对其他账户一律返回空值（显示离线），这是数据层约束，不是渲染约定。国籍同理：勾选「隐藏国籍」后其他用户读到空值，原始 country_code 对非好友根本不返回。

⚠ 一处例外，需要说明：Realtime 的在线状态频道。客户端之间还有一个 presence 频道，用于让好友列表在对方上线时立刻更新。它是全产品共用的一个频道，不按好友关系分片， 因此任何已登录账户订阅它都能看到「现在有哪些账户在线、状态是「在线 / 忙碌」」。能看到的只有 「当前是否在场」这一个事实，看不到历史活跃时间——那个时间戳仍然只对你自己和你的好友可见。

把状态设为「隐身」会让其他人看到你离线，但不会让你从频道的成员列表里消失。我们选择把这个事实写在这里，而不是假装它不存在。

如果你认为「谁现在在线」本身也不该被其他已登录用户看到，请不要使用社区功能——核心检测功能完全不需要登录。

12. 谁能看到什么（含管理员）

  • 其他用户：只能看到你公开的资料——用户名、头像，以及未隐藏时的国籍与「最后活跃」
  • 管理员：能看到你的邮箱、国家代码、平台使用痕迹与登录流水，以及激活码的使用者（用户名 + 邮箱 + 兑换时间）——这是邮箱在服务端唯一会被读取的地方

⚠ 平台痕迹与登录流水只给管理员看：不出现在你的设置页，也不出现在他人主页。承载它们的 platform_logins 表连一条读取策略都没有，只有服务端角色读得到。

13. 聊天室的「撤回」不等于服务端删除

撤回一条消息只是把它从其他人（以及你自己）的界面上移除，并标记为「该消息已被撤回」。原文与附件仍留在服务端，用于举报复核——一个能让发言者随时抹掉记录的房间，等于给滥用者一个「说完就跑」的按钮。

  • 你的客户端会把它从本机缓存里删掉
  • 服务端保留原文供管理员复核；客户端能读到的窗口是最近 7 天

如果你不希望任何发言被服务端保留，请不要使用聊天室——核心检测功能完全不需要登录。

Baishen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05 (with the v1.0.6 release)

In one sentence: the core detection runs entirely on your machine, needs no account, and uploads nothing. Data leaves your computer only after you choose to enter an activation code and you choose to turn cloud sync on.

1. With no account: nothing is uploaded

These work fully offline and make no network request at all:

  • reading the move sequence, running the engine, computing the risk score
  • local archives and the sample library
  • the learning mechanism (automatic threshold search) and the blacklist
  • custom models (weight packages stay in local IndexedDB)

2. What is uploaded once you activate and sign in

ItemContentsWhy
Identityusername, emailthe account itself
Avataravatar (compressed to 256×256)shown on your profile
Account stateactivation status, device ID, last-seenone code per account, 3-device limit
Sample metadatacounts and ratios — aggregates onlythe numbers on the profile tab
Badgeswhich badge types were grantedthe achievement system
Platform traces (1.0.6)extension or web build, the time of each sign-in, the inferred country code (no IP)the operator's census — administrators only
Message read watermarks (1.0.6)the last-read time of the five partitionsthe red dot

3. What is NOT uploaded

  • Your game records are not uploaded — the per-move data stays local
  • Your detection results are not uploaded — scores and per-move verdicts are computed locally
  • Your settings are not uploaded — unless you enable cloud sync
  • Your blacklist is not uploaded — its default is NOT SYNCED; it needs an explicit tick

3.1 Data about your opponents

Samples, replay archives and blacklist rows carry your opponent's display name. With cloud sync on and those categories ticked, those names travel too — they are content you collected, not something we read off a server.

4. Where the data lives

Hosted by Supabase (PostgreSQL + Storage). Singapore (Southeast Asia) is recommended; the actual region depends on the deployment you use.

5. Retention

  • Active accounts: kept until you delete them
  • Deleted accounts: your local data stays where it is, and the cloud data is permanently removed 30 days after you ask
  • Leaked activation codes: an administrator can revoke them in bulk, and revocation does not touch your local data
  • Platform login log: no separate retention window yet — kept alongside the account, deleted with the cloud data within 30 days of account deletion
  • Chat messages: the window a client can read is the last 7 days; files shared into the chat keep a local copy for 15 minutes

6. Your rights

  • Export: the extension's 「导入与导出」 panel exports everything stored locally
  • Delete: 「我的 → 注销账户」 requests deletion of the cloud data (effective after 30 days)
  • Rectify: 「我的 → 修改资料」 edits your username, email and avatar

7. Minors

This extension targets adult players of online gomoku. If you believe the intended audience may include minors, obtain guardian consent before use.

8. Third parties

The service touches the following third parties, and sends data to no one else:

  • Supabase — hosting and storage
  • ipinfo.io — at sign-in the backend sends it your IP address to infer a country code (the IP itself is never stored — see §10)
  • Resend — delivers the verification-code email and the email an administrator sends when replying to a report; your address and the message body pass through it

The detection engines (Rapfi / KataGomo) run locally or at an address you configure, and the optional 「自动提问」 feature uses the LLM endpoint and key you provide — neither passes through us.

9. Contact

Administrator email: mod0207613@qq.com

10. On your own machine: what is stored in the clear

chrome.storage.local is not encrypted. These live as plain text in your browser profile directory, readable by any process that can read that directory:

  • the LLM API key used by 「自动提问」 — an exported backup strips it; the local store does not
  • the cloud account's login token (JWT, valid for 30 days) — deliberately kept outside settings, so neither export/import nor cloud sync carries it; logging out, changing the password or an administrator's revocation invalidates it
  • the chat room's local cache (IndexedDB database baishen-cache) — the last 7 days of public-room messages, one copy of any shared file (15 minutes) and the sync metadata; recalling a message deletes it from this cache

If you do not trust the other programs on this machine, do not enable 「自动提问」 and do not sign in to a cloud account — the core detection needs neither.

11. Who can see your online status and country

Your last-seen timestamp is answered with null for every other account (drawn as 「offline」), and that is enforced in the data, not in the rendering. The same holds for nationality: with 「隐藏国籍」 ticked, other users read null, and the raw country_code is not returned to a non-friend at all.

⚠ One exception, stated plainly: the Realtime presence channel. Clients also share a presence channel so a friend list updates the moment somebody arrives. It is one channel for the whole product, not sharded per friendship, so any signed-in account that subscribes to it can see which accounts are currently present and whether they are 「online」 or 「busy」. What it reveals is presence only; it does not reveal the historical last-seen timestamp, which stays friends-only as described above.

Setting your status to 「隐身」 makes others see you as offline, but it does not remove you from the channel's member list. We would rather write that here than pretend otherwise.

If you consider 「who is online right now」 itself too much to share with other signed-in users, do not use the community features — the core detection needs no account at all.

12. Who can see what (administrators included)

  • Other users see only your public profile — username, avatar, and (unless hidden) your country and last-seen
  • Administrators can see your email, your country code, your platform traces and login log, and who redeemed an activation code (username + email + time) — the only place the backend reads an email address

⚠ Platform traces and the login log are shown to administrators only: they are not on your settings screen and not on anyone's profile. The table that holds them, platform_logins, has no read policy at all — only the service role can reach it.

13. Recalling a chat message is not a server-side delete

Recalling a message only removes it from other people's screens (and yours) and marks it 「该消息已被撤回」. The original text and any attachment stay on the server so a report can be reviewed — a room where a speaker can erase the record at will hands an abuser a 「say it and run」 button.

  • Your client deletes it from the local cache
  • the server keeps the original for an administrator reviewing a report; the window a client can read is the last 7 days

If you would rather no message be retained server-side, do not use the chat — the core detection needs no account at all.
